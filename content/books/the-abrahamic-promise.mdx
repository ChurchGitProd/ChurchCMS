abstract: "This monograph provides a rigorous exegetical examination of the covenant ceremony recorded in Genesis 15. Dr. Vance investigates Ancient Near Eastern treaty patterns, the theological significance of the smoking fire pot and flaming torch passing between the animal pieces, and the unconditional nature of God's unilateral promise. The study tracks how this foundational covenant shapes prophetic anticipation, the Davidic lineage, and Paul's articulation of justification by faith in Galatians and Romans."
