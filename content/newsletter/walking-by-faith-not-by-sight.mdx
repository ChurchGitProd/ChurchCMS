date: "June 29, 2024"
scripture: "2 Corinthians 5:7"
author: "Dr. Thomas Keller"
description: "Faith is not a blind leap into the unknown, but a confident trust in the known character of God. Explore what it means to walk by faith in every aspect of life."
---

Faith is not a blind leap into the unknown, but a confident trust in the known character of God. In his second letter to the Corinthians, Paul reminds us that our present journey is guided by eternal spiritual realities rather than immediate physical circumstances. When trials cloud our vision, we anchor our hope in the steadfast promises of Scripture.
