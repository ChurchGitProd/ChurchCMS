description: "Holiness is not merely moral perfection but being set apart for God's purposes. Learn how the call to holiness shapes our identity and daily walk."
---

Holiness is not merely moral perfection but being set apart for God's purposes. The apostle Peter echoes the Old Testament command: "You shall be holy, for I am holy." This calling touches every facet of our daily life—our thoughts, our relationships, and our service—conforming our character to the image of our Redeemer.
