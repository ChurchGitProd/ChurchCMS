---
title: "Abiding in the Vine"
date: "June 25, 2024"
scripture: "John 15:1-8"
author: "Prof. Sarah Williams"
description: "Jesus uses the imagery of the vine and branches to teach us about spiritual vitality and fruitfulness. Learn what it means to truly abide in Him."
---

Jesus uses the imagery of the vine and branches to teach us about spiritual vitality and fruitfulness. Apart from Christ, we can do nothing of lasting eternal worth. To abide in Him means continually drawing life from His Word, resting in His love, and bearing the fruit of obedience that glorifies the Father.
