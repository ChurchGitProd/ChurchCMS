---

Spiritual warfare is a reality for every believer. Writing from imprisonment, Paul uses the vivid imagery of Roman military equipment to portray our spiritual defense: the belt of truth, the breastplate of righteousness, the shield of faith, the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit. Standing firm requires daily reliance upon God's sovereign might.
