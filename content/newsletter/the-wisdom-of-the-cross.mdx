date: "June 28, 2024"
scripture: "1 Corinthians 1:18"
author: "Prof. Eleanor Sterling"
description: "The message of the cross appears foolish to the world, but to those being saved, it is the power of God. Discover the divine wisdom hidden in Christ's sacrifice."
---

The message of the cross appears foolish to the world, but to those being saved, it is the power of God. Human philosophy prizes outward strength and worldly status, yet God reveals His supreme power through the humility and sacrifice of Calvary. In Christ crucified, divine wisdom dismantles earthly pride and offers redemption to all who believe.
