# Hebrews 8–10: The Superior Sanctuary and New Covenant

> *Note: This content is preserved from the original website prototype and is designated as curriculum study material.*

## Expository Outline

Hebrews 8–10 presents the theological core of the Epistle to the Hebrews, demonstrating the superiority of Christ's eternal priesthood, the true heavenly sanctuary, and the once-for-all sacrifice of the New Covenant.

### Expository Divisions

1. **A Minister in the True Tent (Hebrews 8:1–6)**:
   - Christ as High Priest seated at the right hand of the Majesty in heaven.
   - The earthly tabernacle was merely a shadow and copy of the heavenly pattern showed to Moses on the mountain.

2. **The New Covenant Promised and Inaugurated (Hebrews 8:7–13)**:
   - Quoting Jeremiah 31:31–34 in full to prove the obsolescence of the first covenant and the eternal nature of the new heart, forgiven sins, and unmediated knowledge of God.

3. **Blood of the Eternal Covenant (Hebrews 9:1–28)**:
   - The limitations of animal blood: bulls and goats could only purify the flesh, but the blood of Christ purifies the conscience from dead works to serve the living God.
   - Entering once for all into the holy places, not with the blood of goats and calves, but by means of His own blood.

4. **The Finality of the Sacrifice (Hebrews 10:1–18)**:
   - The repetitive nature of Levitical sacrifices stood as an annual reminder of sin.
   - By a single offering, Christ has perfected for all time those who are being sanctified.
