# Exodus 19–24: The Sinaitic Covenant and Law

> *Note: This content is preserved from the original website prototype and is designated as curriculum study material.*

## Expository Outline

Exodus 19–24 recounts the solemn arrival of Israel at Mount Sinai, the revelation of Yahweh in glory, and the giving of the covenant document that governed Israel as a kingdom of priests.

### Structural Analysis

1. **The Preamble and Divine Deliverance (Exodus 19:1–6)**:
   - "You yourselves have seen what I did to the Egyptians, and how I bore you on eagles' wings and brought you to Myself."
   - The conditional promise of Israel becoming a treasured possession, a kingdom of priests, and a holy nation.

2. **The Decalogue (Exodus 20:1–17)**:
   - The Ten Words delivered directly by God, dividing into duties toward God (Commandments 1–4) and duties toward neighbor (Commandments 5–10).
   - The Decalogue as the perpetual moral reflection of God's holy character.

3. **The Book of the Covenant (Exodus 21–23)**:
   - Case laws (*mishpatim*) applying moral principles to civil, judicial, and social realities of ancient Israel.
   - Sabbatical cycles, annual feasts, and the promise of the Angel of the Lord.

4. **Ratification of the Covenant (Exodus 24:1–18)**:
   - The sprinkling of the blood of the covenant upon the altar and the people.
   - The covenant meal atop Mount Sinai before the God of Israel, where Moses enters the cloud for forty days and nights.
