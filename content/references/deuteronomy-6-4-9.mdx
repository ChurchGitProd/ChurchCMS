## Scripture Focus: Deuteronomy 6:4-9

> "Hear, O Israel: The Lord our God, the Lord is one. You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might."

### Liturgical History of the Shema

Known in Jewish tradition as the *Shema* (from the opening Hebrew imperative שְׁמַע, *Shema*, meaning "Hear" or "Listen attentively and obey"), this passage forms the core confession of monotheism and covenant allegiance in the Hebrew Bible.

#### The Triple Sphere of Devotion
- **Heart (לֵבָב, *levav*)**: The seat of intellect, volition, conscience, and purpose.
- **Soul (נֶפֶשׁ, *nephesh*)**: The vital breath, life force, desires, and full emotional being.
- **Might (מְאֹד, *me'od*)**: Literally "muchness"—one's capacity, material resources, and strength pushed to its utmost limit.

### Christological Synthesis in Mark 12:28-34

When a scribe inquired of Jesus, *"Which commandment is the most important of all?"*, Jesus responded not by instituting a novel decree, but by quoting Deuteronomy 6:4-5 verbatim, immediately coupling it with Leviticus 19:18 (*"You shall love your neighbor as yourself"*):

1. **Unity of Love of God and Neighbor**: Jesus demonstrates that genuine devotion to the one true God necessarily flows into ethical righteousness and compassionate community conduct.
2. **Fulfillment of the Law**: Paul later echoes this synthesis in Romans 13:8-10, declaring that love is the fulfillment of the whole Torah.
